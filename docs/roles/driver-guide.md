# Driver guide

A Driver is assigned to a vehicle and handles the actual trip execution. Their work is to confirm attendance, record stop taps in order, submit the completion, and report trip problems when needed.

## What a Driver can do

- See only the approved trips assigned to their vehicle
- Tap Start trip at ANONANG when leaving base
- Tap End trip when returning to base
- Tap Arrived and Leaving at each stop in sequence
- Submit one odometer pair after the trip is complete
- Report a problem on a trip at any time
- View their own trip history and schedule status

## Starting points

- My schedule: the approved trips assigned to you
- Current trip card: the active trip in progress
- Attendance and stop-tap workflow: record each leg of the route

## Typical workflow

### 1. Start the trip

At departure:
1. Open the assigned schedule in My schedule.
2. Tap Start trip.
3. Confirm the tap is recorded with your location when available.

The system captures the time and may flag the tap if the location was weak or outside the base area.

### 2. Record stops while driving

As you reach each destination or stop:
- tap Arrived when you arrive at the stop
- tap Leaving when you depart the stop

These taps define the actual time-out and time-in entries used on the completed trip.

### 3. End the trip

When you return to base:
1. Tap End trip.
2. Submit the trip completion when the trip is over.
3. Enter the odometer readings and any end-of-trip details required by the process.

The system expects one odometer pair for the whole schedule.

### 4. Report an issue when needed

A Driver can report a problem on the trip at any time, even before submission. This is not a replacement for the official Admin review; it surfaces the issue for follow-up.

## Key guardrails

- A Driver sees only trips assigned to their own vehicle.
- A trip cannot be completed without the required attendance steps.
- Completion is locked after submission; a Driver cannot edit the final trip record after it is submitted.
- Only an Admin may correct times, handle formal problem resolution, or mark a trip Done.

## Best practices

- Tap Start trip before leaving base.
- Record each stop as you go rather than after the fact.
- Submit the completion promptly once the trip has ended and the readings are confirmed.
- If a stop or trip issue needs admin attention, report it immediately so it is not missed.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] See assigned trip in My schedule
- [ ] Tap Start trip before leaving
- [ ] Tap Arrived and Leaving at each stop
- [ ] Tap End trip on return
- [ ] Submit odometer readings and completion details
- [ ] Report problems if one occurs
